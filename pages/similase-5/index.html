What does "GFCF" mean?

GFCF stands for gluten-free, casein-free, a way of eating that avoids gluten (from wheat, barley, and rye) and casein (the main protein in milk). Similase GFCF is designed to support people following that lifestyle, with DPP IV activity for the digestion of gluten and casein dipeptides.*

Is it safe for celiac disease or a food allergy?

No supplement replaces a medical diet. From the label: "While Similase GFCF will reduce the level of reactive gliadin and gluten proteins in a meal, it is advised that celiac disease sufferers continue with their normal gluten-exclusion diet as even small amounts of gliadin can cause adverse reactions in the most sensitized individuals." If you have a food allergy, keep avoiding that food.
