The 1-Page Empire · Buyer Conversion Map: Package

Build the offer that gets them to buy now, and buy a lot.

The Package answers the buyer's question: "Why now?" It's the complete offer (product, pricing, bonuses, terms and guarantee) built to get customers to buy now and buy a lot upfront, because most customers only ever buy once.

Browse the 19 offers Real brands · one-time and subscription

How to use this planner

  1. Browse the swipe file and filter to offers that fit your product. Consumables suit subscription offers; durables suit multi-packs and bundles.
  2. Pick one as your base offer. It loads a starting tier structure into the builder.
  3. Enter your real price, landed cost and maximum discount, then shape the tiers, gifts, subscription and guarantee.
  4. Check the margin warnings, then copy the summary and the list of discount codes to create.

Test one change at a time against your current page, and judge on profit per visitor (conversion rate × average order value × margin, plus repeat for subscriptions), not conversion rate alone.
